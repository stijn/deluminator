A light/dark/auto switch with a circular reveal from the switch and a choice of dark palettes.

== Description ==

Dark mode sucks the light into the switch; light mode lets it back out. Three states: automatic (follows the device), light and dark. Choose the dark palette under Settings › Deluminator: Solarized Dark, WCAG Dark or Dracula. A theme with its own dark mode keeps it, and the switch controls it.
